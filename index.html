Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the MathVoice Hub app for mobile devices (a voice-enabled calculator application), together with any related services operated by Hussain (collectively, the "Application"). Hussain is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


No Account Required

The Application does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal identity information (such as your name or email address) in order to use its core features. All calculator functionality, including voice input, works without registration.


No Backend Servers

The Service Provider does not operate any backend servers and does not directly collect, store, or process the information described in this policy on servers owned or controlled by the Service Provider. Where data is collected, it is collected and processed directly by the third-party services described below (Google AdMob, Google Play Services, and RevenueCat), sent directly from your device to those providers.


Information Collected by Third-Party Services

Through the third-party SDKs integrated into the Application, the following information may be collected:

  • Advertising ID (used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads)
  • Device ID and general device/technical information (operating system, device model, IP address)
  • Voice/audio input (processed transiently for speech-to-text; see "Voice Input" below)
  • Purchase history (processed by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for in-app purchases)

The Service Provider does not require or request personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical address, or age in order to use the Application.


Locally Stored Data

Your calculation history (expressions and results) and app preferences (such as theme and voice settings) are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (Hive/SharedPreferences). This data is never transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party, and is not accessible to the Service Provider. You can delete this data at any time by using the "Clear History" option within the Application, or by uninstalling the Application, which removes all locally stored data from your device.


Voice Input

When you use the voice input feature, your spoken audio is processed by your device's built-in speech-recognition service (provided by Google on Android, or Apple on iOS), which converts your speech to text. The Application does not operate its own voice-processing servers and does not store or retain your audio recordings. Only the resulting calculation (for example, "12 + 5") is saved to your device's local history — never the voice recording or the raw transcript. Microphone access is optional; the Application's keypad functions fully without granting microphone permission.


Advertising

The Application displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to serve ads that are relevant to you, and to measure ad performance. You can limit ad personalization or reset your Advertising ID at any time through your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android, or Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS). Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will request your consent before serving personalized ads.


In-App Purchases

The Application offers in-app purchases, processed through Google Play Billing and managed using RevenueCat, a subscription and purchase management platform. RevenueCat processes your purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier to validate purchases and prevent fraud; it does not receive your payment card details, which are handled entirely by Google Play. RevenueCat acts as a data processor on the Service Provider's behalf. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at the link below for further detail.


Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application's third-party SDKs (such as AdMob) may use device identifiers, SDKs, and similar technologies to support ad delivery, analytics, or fraud prevention. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.


Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data processed in connection with the Application. To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, contact the Service Provider at hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider at hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com.


Third Party Access

The Service Provider does not sell your personal information. Aggregated, anonymized data may be used by the third-party services described in this policy to improve their own services. Each third-party service processes data under its own privacy policy, linked below.


International Data Transfers

The Service Provider's third-party service providers may transfer personal data to countries outside your country of residence, including outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, appropriate mechanisms are used, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms
  • Your consent, where required and legally permitted

Opt-Out Rights and Data Deletion

You can stop all data collection by the Application at any time by uninstalling it — since the Application stores no data on the Service Provider's servers, uninstalling removes your local history and preferences completely. To specifically opt out of personalized advertising, use your device's Advertising ID settings referenced above. To request deletion of any purchase-related data held by RevenueCat, or to ask any question about your data, contact the Service Provider at hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com.


How to Delete Your Data

Because the Application has no user accounts and no backend server, there is no single central database of your data to delete from. Instead, each type of data is deleted as follows:

  • Calculation history (stored locally on your device): Delete instantly at any time using the "Clear History" option within the Application, or delete everything by uninstalling the Application.
  • Advertising ID: This identifier is held by Google, not the Service Provider. Reset or opt out of it directly through your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android, or Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS).
  • Device ID and technical data: Also held by Google as part of the AdMob and Google Play Services SDKs, governed by Google's own data controls.
  • Voice/audio input: Nothing to delete — this is never stored by the Application or any third party, even temporarily.
  • Purchase history: Held by RevenueCat and Google Play as data processors on the Service Provider's behalf. Email the Service Provider at hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com with your deletion request, and the Service Provider will submit a deletion request to RevenueCat on your behalf. You may also contact RevenueCat or Google Play directly.

For any data type not listed above, or if you are unsure how to proceed, email hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com and the Service Provider will assist you or direct your request to the relevant third-party provider. Requests are typically actioned within 30 days.


Data Retention Policy

Because the Application has no backend server, the Service Provider does not itself retain your personal data. Data retention for information processed by third-party services is governed by each provider's own policy:

  • Locally stored history and preferences: retained on your device until you clear them or uninstall the Application
  • Advertising ID and ad-related data: retained by Google AdMob per its own retention policy
  • Purchase history: retained by RevenueCat and Google Play per their own retention policies

Children

The Application is a general-purpose calculator suitable for users of all ages, including students and children who may use it for schoolwork. The Application does not require account creation and does not knowingly collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information from any user, regardless of age. However, because the Application displays advertising through Google AdMob and offers in-app purchases, the Service Provider recommends that users under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in their jurisdiction) use the Application under the guidance of a parent or guardian, particularly with respect to advertisements and purchases. Parents or guardians who believe a child has made an unauthorized purchase, or have questions about advertising shown to a child, may contact the Service Provider at hussainxdeveloper@gmail.com.
